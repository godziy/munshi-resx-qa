LIMITED QA TEST · PRIVACY NOTICE

Test app privacy.

Munshi ResX QA is a development app operated by Chayan Ranpara for testing a fictional restaurant. It is not an operating restaurant, ordering service or production customer service. Participation is limited to the operator and explicitly consenting test participants.

Information used

Tests can process a participant’s WhatsApp number and profile name, messages they deliberately send, message identifiers, timestamps and delivery events. The owner workspace uses account and outlet identifiers, supplied restaurant facts, approval decisions and test results. An authorized Google connection reads Search Console reports for the configured demo property. Google’s read-only consent scope can cover the signed-in account’s verified sites; the local application binds this connection to the demo property.

This information is used to verify sign-in, message routing and replies, access controls, website inspection and reporting. Marked test enquiries are excluded from business-result counts. Please use invented restaurant details and do not send sensitive personal information.

Services and storage

WhatsApp messages pass through Meta. During an authorized test session, webhook traffic passes through a temporary Cloudflare tunnel to a local development server. Google supplies authorized Search Console data. GitHub Pages hosts the public demo and this notice. These providers handle traffic under their own policies. Owner requests submitted to the AI assistant are sent to the configured OpenRouter model provider; creating a demo profile does not itself submit an AI request.

Test records are stored in the operator’s local development database. Provider connection tokens are encrypted in that database; private local setup files can also contain credentials. Temporary tunnels and test credentials have limited lifetimes. Expiry of access does not delete earlier test records. The operator handles access and deletion requests manually after verifying the requester. There is no automatic test-record deletion period, and the operator will explain any records that must be kept for an applicable obligation.

Your choices and contact

Participation is voluntary. Stop sending test messages at any time. You can revoke Google account access in your Google Account connections. To request access to or deletion of your test information, or to report a privacy concern, contact chayanranparacr@gmail.com. Identify the test and your request; do not email passwords or access tokens. The operator will verify the request before disclosing or deleting records.

This notice describes the limited QA setup as of 27 September 2026. It is not a statement that the broader Munshi product is ready for production. Production use requires a separately reviewed privacy notice and operational controls.
